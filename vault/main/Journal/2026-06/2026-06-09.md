## 12:31 — Watchdog timeout message sent to wrong chat (v3.72.2)

- User received "⏰ Timeout — Claude está sem responder há 1202s" in the wrong Telegram chat without having sent anything.
- Root cause: `_watchdog` thread has its own thread-local `_ctx`, which is never seeded — causing `self._chat_id` to fall back to the primary `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` regardless of which chat originated the runner.
- The timeout was actually from the `ai-digest-v2/collect-and-curate` step that ran since 11:12 and stalled silently at ~12:09.
- Fix: capture `chat_id` and `thread_id` from `ctx` at thread spawn time (`_wd_chat_id`, `_wd_thread_id`) and pass explicitly to all `send_message()` calls inside `_watchdog`. For ctx=None (routines), fallback to primary chat is preserved.
- Bumped to v3.72.2, committed.
